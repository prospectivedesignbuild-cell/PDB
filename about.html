Who We Are

Founded by Pramod Kumar Singh with over a decade of specialized industry leadership in decorative laminates, architectural surfaces, plywood, and turnkey interior contracting, Prospective Design And Build brings corporate-grade accountability to interior execution.

Whether executing a luxury apartment or villa in Lucknow, a banquet complex in Ranchi, or a high-security showroom in Pilibhit, every project follows documented 3D visualizations, transparent Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and fixed milestone timelines.

  • BWP Marine-Grade Plywood And Certified Architectural Surfaces
  • Zero Hidden Cost Escalations with Itemized BOQ
  • Dedicated Site Supervision And Quality Checklists
How We Work

Our 4-Stage Execution Framework

01

Site Audit And Brief

Laser measurement, structural inspection, MEP assessment, and lifestyle or operational requirement mapping.

02

3D Design And BOQ

Photorealistic 3D renders, material finish boards, and line-by-line transparent costing before work begins.

03

Precision Build

Civil modifications, concealed electrical/plumbing, false ceiling, and factory-finished modular woodwork.

04

Snag-Free Handover

Deep cleaning, hardware calibration, lighting checks, and documented turnkey handover on schedule.
