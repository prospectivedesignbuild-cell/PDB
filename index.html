Where We Work

Interior Design & Turnkey Projects in Lucknow

Prospective Design And Build is an interior design and execution business based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. The company works on residential homes, luxury villas, independent kothis, apartments, offices, retail showrooms and hospitality environments.

Project services may include space planning, design concepts, 3D visualisation, material and finish selection, modular work, site coordination, interior execution and handover, according to the agreed project scope.

For projects outside Lucknow, execution feasibility, travel, logistics and site coordination are assessed individually before a proposal is prepared.
