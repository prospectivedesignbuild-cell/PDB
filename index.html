Frequently Asked Questions

What is the turnkey interior design cost per sq.ft?

Turnkey interior packages start at ₹1,450 per sq.ft for Essential Turnkey, ₹1,950 per sq.ft for Premium Acrylic and Veneer finishes, and ₹2,650 per sq.ft for Signature Luxury with home automation.

Which cities does Prospective Design And Build serve?

Headquartered in Gomti Nagar Extension, Lucknow, we deliver turnkey residential, luxury villa, commercial, and hospitality projects across Pan-India including Noida NCR, Ranchi, Bareilly, Pilibhit, Ayodhya, and Madhubani.

What is the standard project completion timeline?

Structured turnkey interior handovers are completed within 45 to 90 days depending on carpet area and civil scope, backed by documented 3D designs and itemized BOQs.
